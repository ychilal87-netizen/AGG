How It Works

1. Place a Stake: Input your preferred amount before the round begins.

2. Flight Phase: Once takeoff starts, the multiplier ascends from 1.00x upwards exponentially.

3. Cash Out: Secure your winnings by pressing "Cash Out" before the airplane flies away (Crashes).

4. Profit Calculation: Your payout is calculated by multiplying your bet by the current multiplier. For example, a bet of 100 ETB cashed out at 2.50x yields 250 ETB!

Crucial Rule: If the airplane flies away (Crashes) before you exit, your stake is fully lost.
